TrendyAI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

TrendyAI is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single individual. It helps the operator collect fashion trend inspiration on their own Pinterest account.

What the app does

TrendyAI reads publicly available fashion trend information and the operator's own Pinterest boards. It then creates boards and Pins on the operator's own Pinterest account only.

Data we access

The app accesses only the operator's own Pinterest account, using the permissions the operator grants through Pinterest's official login (OAuth). It does not access, collect, or store data from any other Pinterest user.

How data is used and stored

Pinterest data is used solely to create and organize the operator's own boards and Pins. A small log of which Pins were created is stored in the operator's private code repository. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Trend keywords may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to decide which trends to save; no personal data of other users is included.

Your choices

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest's settings under connected apps.
